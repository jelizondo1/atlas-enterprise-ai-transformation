# APEX INDUSTRIAL SUPPLY
## Supplier Services & Materials Agreement

**Supplier ID:** V-203811  
**Agreement ID:** APEX-MRG-2023-16  
**Effective:** February 1, 2023  
**Strategic supplier:** No  
**Annual Meridian spend:** approximately $48M

Apex supplies maintenance materials, store fixtures, equipment parts, installation services, and emergency repair services.

### Materials pricing
Material purchases are governed by the PO. Minor unit-price discrepancies may be accepted under Meridian's standard tolerance policy.

### Quantity tolerance — consumable materials
For consumables sold by weight, length, or bulk packaging, invoiced quantity may vary from PO quantity by up to **2%** without a PO amendment when receiving confirms delivered quantity, unit price is unchanged, and incremental invoice amount is below $5,000.

### Installation services
Installation labor may be invoiced after the responsible Facilities Manager approves completion. A goods receipt is not required when a valid electronic service confirmation exists.

### Combined invoices
Apex may submit one invoice containing materials and installation services. Each portion must be validated independently. A missing service confirmation does not automatically prevent payment of valid materials, and a materials discrepancy does not automatically invalidate approved installation charges.

### Emergency work
Apex may perform emergency repair work before formal PO creation when necessary to prevent store closure, safety risk, refrigeration loss, or material operational disruption. The Meridian Facilities Director must provide written confirmation within five business days. AP may not approve based solely on Apex's statement that emergency authorization occurred.

### Freight
Apex shipments are generally **COLLECT**. Separate supplier freight charges should not be paid unless an individual PO explicitly authorizes supplier-arranged transportation.

### Corrected invoices and credit notes
Supplier overbilling normally requires a credit note/corrected invoice. On mixed invoices, Meridian may process the supported portion while awaiting correction of the disputed balance.

### Service-hour disputes
Invoiced labor hours up to 5% above the approved work order may be paid when the Facilities Manager confirms additional hours were required. Above 5% requires Facilities and Procurement approval.

### Dispute window
Standard Meridian 90-day window applies.
