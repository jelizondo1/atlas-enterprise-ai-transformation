# NORTHSTAR CONSUMER PRODUCTS
## Master Supplier Agreement — Commercial Terms Extract

**Supplier ID:** V-100184  
**Agreement ID:** MSA-NS-2019-047  
**Effective:** July 1, 2019  
**Strategic supplier:** Yes  
**Annual Meridian spend:** approximately $420M

### Pricing
NorthStar merchandise is invoiced according to PO pricing unless modified by an executed promotional or pricing addendum. Valid promotional agreements supersede PO price for the applicable SKU, geography, quantity, and effective period.

### Promotional allowances
Promotional discounts may be off-invoice or post-period rebate. AP should not apply a post-period rebate as an invoice deduction unless the promotional agreement expressly permits it.

### Freight — original agreement
Unless otherwise stated on the PO, shipments are **PREPAID_INCLUDED**. NorthStar may not separately invoice standard freight.

### Proof of delivery
NorthStar proof of delivery does not override documented shortages, damages, or receiving discrepancies.

### Payment disputes
NorthStar may dispute deductions or underpayments within **120 calendar days**. This supersedes Meridian's standard 90-day window.

### Strategic-supplier coordination
Procurement must be consulted before final supplier communication when AP would reject a claim above $100,000, materially change historical treatment, or interpret a promotional agreement disputed by NorthStar.

## Amendment 2 — Freight Modification
**Agreement ID:** MSA-NS-2019-047-A2  
**Effective:** January 1, 2024

For shipments originating from NorthStar distribution centers in **Dallas, Phoenix, and Joliet**, freight terms are **PREPAID_ADD**. NorthStar may separately invoice actual transportation cost subject to supporting carrier documentation.

All other origins remain subject to the original MSA freight terms.
