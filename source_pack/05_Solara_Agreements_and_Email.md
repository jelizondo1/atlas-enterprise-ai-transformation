### Currency
U.S.-destination POs are normally USD. Mexico-destination POs are normally MXN. Currency changes require an approved PO amendment.

### Incoterms — original agreement
Unless otherwise specified:
- Mexico → U.S.: **FCA Monterrey**
- Domestic Mexico: **DDP Meridian destination**

Under FCA Monterrey, Solara delivers goods to the designated carrier and Meridian assumes primary transportation after carrier acceptance.

### U.S. import charges
For U.S.-destination merchandise, Meridian is responsible for primary cross-border freight, customs brokerage, and applicable import duties unless the PO explicitly states otherwise. Solara may not add these charges to its merchandise invoice unless previously approved.

### Domestic Mexico freight
Under DDP, transportation and delivery costs are included in merchandise price. Separate freight charges should not be paid.

### Tax
Mexico domestic invoices must satisfy applicable tax-documentation requirements. Ambiguous cross-border VAT, withholding, or customs-tax questions go to Tax.

### Quantity disputes
For imported merchandise, final receiving quantity governs. Carrier acceptance at origin does not establish final accepted quantity.

## Amendment 1 — Cross-Border Freight Pilot
**Agreement ID:** SOL-MRG-2022-09-A1  
**Effective:** January 1, 2025 through June 30, 2025

For shipments from Solara's Monterrey DC to Meridian DCs in Texas, Solara will arrange transportation under **PREPAID_ADD**. Solara may separately invoice actual freight plus a maximum administrative fee of 3% of carrier cost. Carrier invoices are required for freight above $2,500.

Unless extended in writing, underlying FCA terms automatically resume after expiration.
