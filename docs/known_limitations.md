# Known limitations

- Fictional Meridian enterprise and synthetic data; no actual customer integration or deployment.
- No API credential available at packaging: zero live attempts, zero live semantic reviews, no selected model, no token/cost or quality result.
- Twelve visible development cases, not a fresh unseen benchmark. Published controls and source data preclude a claim of blind evaluation.
- Local reviewer identity and independence are self-attested. Hash chains and source hashes are not authenticated signatures, authorization or tamper-proof audit infrastructure.
- No autonomous approval, payment, rejection, hold clearance or supplier communication. Session reviews are evidence assessments only.
- Solara 2026 authority, Apex disputed scope, exact $50,000 policy overlap and historical effective-date gaps remain unresolved.
- Opportunity workload associations overlap and are not causal benefits. ROI inputs remain unknown until measurements, adoption, maintenance, costs and realization plans exist.
- Demo component figures are explicitly sourced design values for the supplied Apex packet; they are not a general invoice extraction engine.
- A future production build needs identity/RBAC, secrets management, privacy/security review, policy versioning, access-scoped retrieval, durable multi-user audit, resilient ERP/document integrations, monitoring, change control and domain-approved release gates.
- Python 3.12 and Windows were exercised locally. Other operating systems use portable paths but have not been executed here. Initial dependency installation needs network access.
